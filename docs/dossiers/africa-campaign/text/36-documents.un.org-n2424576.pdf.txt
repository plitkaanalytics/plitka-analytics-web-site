Letter dated 19 August 2024 from the representatives of Burkina
Faso, Mali and Niger to the United Nations addressed to the
President of the Security Council

       Upon instructions from our authorities, we have the honour to transmit herewith
a joint letter, dated 19 August 2024, from Karamoko Jean-Marie Traoré, Minister for
Foreign Affairs, Regional Cooperation and the Diaspora of Burkina Faso; Abdoulaye
Diop, Minister for Foreign Affairs and International Cooperation of the Republic of
Mali; and Bakary Yaou Sangaré, Minister for Foreign Affairs, Cooperation and the
Diaspora of the Republic of Niger, addressed to you (see annex).

       In the joint letter, the Ministers for Foreign Affairs of Burkina Faso, Mali and
Niger strongly denounce and condemn the open and assumed support of the
Government of the Republic of Ukraine for international terrorism, particularly in the
Sahel. They call on the Security Council to assume its responsibilities with regard to
the deliberate choice by Ukraine to support terrorism, in order to prevent such
subversive actions which threaten the stability of the Sahel and even the African
continent.

       We should be grateful if you would have the present letter and its annex brought
to the attention of the members of the Security Council and issued as a document of
the Council.

                   On behalf of our respective Governments, we are transmitting to you this letter
            to denounce the open and assumed support by the Government of the Republic of
            Ukraine for international terrorism, particularly in the Sahel.

                   Indeed, we were unpleasantly surprised by the subversive comments made by
            Mr. Andry Yusov, spokesperson for the Ukrainian Military Intelligence Agency,
            admitting the involvement of Ukraine in the cowardly, traitorous and barbaric attack
            by armed terrorist groups on 24, 25 and 26 July 2024, which caused the death of
            members of the Malian defense and security forces in Tinzawatene, Mali, as well as
            significant property damage.

                   These statements were confirmed by Mr. Yurii Pyvovarov, Ambassador of Ukraine
            to Senegal, who openly and without the slightest ambiguity expressed his country ’s
            support for international terrorism, particularly in the Sahel. Worse, these high -ranking
            Ukrainian officials have announced publicly that other “results” are still to come.

                   Such statements, which are extremely serious, represent much more than
            interference, which in itself is reprehensible. They demonstrate official and
            unequivocal support by the Ukrainian Government for terrorism in Africa,
            particularly in the Sahel. These acts also constitute a violation of the sovereignty and
            territorial integrity of our States, gross aggression against our countries and support
            for international terrorism, in flagrant violation of the Charter of the United Nations
            and its related international conventions.

                   We therefore strongly condemn the Ukrainian authorities’ aggression and
            glorification of terrorism, which nothing can justify. We call on the Security Council
            to assume its responsibilities with regard to the deliberate choice by Ukraine to
            support terrorism, a scourge unanimously rejected internationally in all its forms and
            manifestations.

                   Emphasizing that the acts for which Ukraine, the aggressor State, has officially
            claimed responsibility pose a direct threat to international peace and security,
            particularly in the Sahel region and in Africa, we call on the Security Council, which
            bears the primary responsibility for maintaining international peace and security, to
            take appropriate actions against these subversive acts which strengthen terrorist
            groups in Africa and demonstrate the involvement of foreign State sponsors in the
            spread of terrorism in the region, an issue which our States have regularly denounced.

                   These acts of aggression, far from achieving their objective, reinforce the
            commitment and determination of Burkina Faso, the Republic of Mali and the
            Republic of Niger, within the framework of the Confederation of Sahel States, to
            pursue more vigorously their resolute march towards strengthening their sovereignty
            and taking control of their destiny, in accordance with the deep aspirations of the
            people of the Confederation of Sahel States.
