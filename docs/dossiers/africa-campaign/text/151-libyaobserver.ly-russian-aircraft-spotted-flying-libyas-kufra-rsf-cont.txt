Russian aircraft spotted flying from Libya’s Kufra to RSF-controlled areas in Sudan
by
LibyaMohammed
Thu, 17/07/2025 - 11:37
Share
Tweet
Two Russian cargo planes were recently tracked flying from Kufra Airport in southeastern Libya to areas controlled by the Rapid Support Forces (RSF) in Sudan, according to satellite images from the European Copernicus Program reported by Italy’s Nova news agency.
The aircraft, IL-76 is typically used for transporting military personnel and equipment, as well as for medium-range logistical operations.
Nova reported that the RSF has been consolidating its presence in the strategic tri-border region between Sudan, Libya, and Egypt. This area has become a critical logistical hub for supplying RSF forces in Darfur and Kordofan.
The agency also pointed to a noticeable increase in arms shipments from eastern Libya to the RSF since the end of May, reportedly orchestrated by the United Arab Emirates.
Kufra Airport plays a key operational role, serving as a logistical base to facilitate the continued flow of military supplies to RSF forces through remote and lightly monitored corridors.
Similar movements had previously been documented by CNN, which analyzed flight tracking data showing multiple cargo flights transporting military and logistical equipment from southern Libya to RSF-controlled areas in Sudan late last December.
