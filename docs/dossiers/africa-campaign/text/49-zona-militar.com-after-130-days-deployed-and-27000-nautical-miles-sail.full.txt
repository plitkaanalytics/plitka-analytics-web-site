After 130 days deployed and 27,000 nautical miles sailed, Russian Navy frigate Admiral Kasatonov returns to Severomorsk

After
130 days of deployment
and more than
27,000 nautical miles sailed
, the
Russian Navy frigate Admiral Kasatonov
returned on Monday to its homeport of
Severomorsk
, in the Murmansk region. The arrival brought to a close a lengthy mission that saw the ship operate across several maritime areas before rejoining the Northern Fleet at its principal base.

Upon arrival in Severomorsk, the crew was welcomed by
Northern Fleet Commander Admiral Konstantin Kabantsov
, who presented medals and certificates to personnel who took part in the deployment. In keeping with a Russian Navy tradition, the ship’s commanding officer was also presented with a roasted piglet as part of the homecoming ceremony.
The frigate Admiral Kasatonov during its visit to Algeria
Kabantsov said the frigate had carried out
strategic deterrence missions in operationally important areas
, protected Russian maritime economic activity, helped safeguard freedom of navigation and contributed to strengthening ties with countries regarded by Moscow as partners. He also highlighted the crew’s training, coordination and professionalism, as well as the reliability of the ship’s weapons and onboard systems throughout the deployment.

According to the Northern Fleet commander, all tasks assigned to Admiral Kasatonov were completed successfully despite what he described as a period of heightened geopolitical tensions for Russia. Kabantsov also indicated that the frigate could undertake further long-range missions in the near future.
Although the Russian Navy did not disclose every port call and operating area visited during the 130-day mission, one of the most notable stops took place in
Oran, Algeria
, where Admiral Kasatonov was accompanied by the medium sea tanker
Akademik Pashin
. The visit once again highlighted the close defence relationship between Moscow and Algiers, at a time when Algeria operates a submarine fleet made up of
Kilo and Kilo II-class boats
and has recently begun
fielding
Su-57E stealth fighters
, becoming the first foreign operator of the aircraft.
Another significant stage of the deployment came when Admiral Kasatonov transited the
Strait of Gibraltar
into the Atlantic. Following its departure, the presence of Russian warships in the Mediterranean was interrupted, something that had not occurred since
2013
.
That development is linked in large part to the loss of access to the Syrian port of
Tartus
as a permanent support base for the Russian Navy, as well as Turkey’s decision to keep the
Bosporus and Dardanelles straits
closed to warships involved in the Russia-Ukraine war under the provisions of the
Montreux Convention
.
At the same time, the Russian Navy continues to expand its fleet of
Project 22350 frigates
. Admiral Kasatonov is currently one of three ships of the series in service alongside
Admiral Gorshkov
and
Admiral Golovko
.
The fleet is expected to grow further with the future addition of
Admiral Gromov
, whose construction began in May this year.
