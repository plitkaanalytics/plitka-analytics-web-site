Ukrainian Foreign Ministry denies supplying drones to Libya
by
AbdulkaderAssad
Thu, 28/08/2025 - 19:00
Share
Tweet
The Ukrainian Foreign Ministry has denied claims made by a Russian official that Kyiv had sent drones and trainers to Libya and other African countries to coordinate terrorist attacks.
In a statement reported by the Ukrainian Embassy in Libya, the ministry said the Russian allegations were baseless and part of a systematic disinformation campaign against Ukraine.
The ministry further stressed that Moscow was accusing others of practices it carried out itself, such as sending mercenaries, looting resources, and destabilizing Africa.
Russian media reports had earlier claimed that the Government of National Unity had used Ukrainian drones to launch attacks against Russian forces stationed in Libya, in areas under Haftar’s control.
The reports, which the embassy rejected, also alleged that Ukraine had supplied Libya with "Mavic 3" drones equipped with Ukrainian-made launch systems and was training Libyan personnel to operate them.
