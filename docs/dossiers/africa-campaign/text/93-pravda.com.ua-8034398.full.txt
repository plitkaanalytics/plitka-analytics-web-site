No evidence that drone found off Greek coast belongs to Ukrainian operators, Foreign Ministry says
Khrystyna Bondarieva ,
Alona Mazurenko
—
                12 May, 20:52
Photo: Ekathimerini
Посилання скопійовано
Add UP as preferred source in Google
Heorhii Tykhyi, spokesman for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine, has said there is no evidence that a maritime drone discovered last week off the coast of Greece is linked to Ukrainian operators.
Source:
Tykhyi in a comment to journalists;
European Pravda
Details:
Tykhyi said Ukraine currently has no information about the uncrewed surface vessel found in Greece.
Advertisement:
Quote:
"There is no evidence that it belongs to Ukrainian naval drone operators."
More details:
Tykhyi added that Ukraine is open to cooperating with Greece to establish the circumstances of the incident, if the Greek side submits appropriate requests.
Background:
The Guardian
reported
earlier that Greek authorities have stepped up an investigation into how a drone carrying explosives ended up in waters off the country's west coast.
Ukrainian defence technology company Uforce clarified in a comment to Ukrainska Pravda that the vessel was not a Magura drone, as some media outlets had previously reported.
Ekathimerini, a Greek media outlet, had
claimed
earlier that a Ukrainian Magura 5 drone had been found in waters south of the Ionian island of Lefkada.
