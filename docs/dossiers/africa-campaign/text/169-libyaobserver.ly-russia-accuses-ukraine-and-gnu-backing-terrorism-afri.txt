Russia accuses Ukraine and GNU of backing terrorism in Africa’s Sahel
by
SafaAlharathy
Thu, 09/10/2025 - 14:03
Share
Tweet
Russia has accused Ukraine of supporting terrorist groups in Africa’s Sahel region, allegedly in coordination with Libya’s Government of National Unity (GNU).
Foreign Ministry spokesperson Maria Zakharova told reporters on Wednesday that Kyiv’s “terrorism knows no borders,” claiming Ukraine’s military intelligence had supplied attack drones and training to militants in the region through links with Libya’s security forces, allegedly brokered by the UK.
Zakharova said Moscow had evidence of cooperation between Ukrainian forces and Libya’s GNU in organising “terrorist operations” in countries including Niger. She also cited Sudanese military claims that several Ukrainian mercenaries fighting alongside the Rapid Support Forces were recently killed in Darfur.
The Russian official further accused Ukraine of illegally selling Western-supplied weapons to armed groups across Africa and the Middle East, naming Burkina Faso, Mali, Niger, Somalia, Syria, and Chad among others.
