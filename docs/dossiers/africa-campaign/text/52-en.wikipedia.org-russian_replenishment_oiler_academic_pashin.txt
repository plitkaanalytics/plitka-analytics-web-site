* Academic Pashin* is a 

[replenishment oiler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Replenishment_oiler)of

[Project 23130](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_23130_replenishment_oiler)and the lead vessel of the class. Developed by the Spetssudoproect JSC, it is the first replenishment oiler commissioned with the

[Russian Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Navy)since the end of the

[Cold War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cold_War).

The ship is named after the

[[1]](#cite_note-ship-technology-1)[Hero of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_the_Russian_Federation)

[Valentin Pashin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentin_Pashin).

She is assigned to the

[[2]](#cite_note-2)[Northern Fleet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Fleet).

## History

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Russian_replenishment_oiler_Academic_Pashin&action=edit§ion=1)]

The construction of *Academic Pashin* was carried out under a state contract for the [Russian Defence Ministry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Defence_(Russia)), signed in November 2013. [1] The ship was laid down on 26 May 2014, during a keel lying ceremony at the Nevsky Shipyard in 

[Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg). It was first scheduled for delivery by late 2016.


[[3]](#cite_note-3)On 18 March 2016, the ship's hull was rolled out of the construction hall, to start pre-finishing works before its launching on the water. [4] It was launched on 26 May 2016.


[[5]](#cite_note-5)On 17 May 2018, it has started first stage of its sea trials in the [Ladoga Lake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ladoga_Lake). [6] In September 2018, the ship arrived in 

[Kronstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kronstadt), where it was to join the Russian Navy by the year end.


[[7]](#cite_note-7)

[[8]](#cite_note-8)In early 2019, the trials continued in the [Baltic Sea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_Sea), where the ship's interaction with deck helicopters was worked out. The [Ka-27PS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamov_Ka-27#Variants) helicopters were delivering various cargoes onto the ship and back to shore.[[9]](#cite_note-9)*Academic Pashin* successfully completed the trials in late June 2019, [10] and was commissioned on 21 January 2020.


[[11]](#cite_note-11)In April 2020, *Academic Pashin* was spotted in the [Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Channel) off [Cherbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherbourg_Naval_Base) during the first sea trials of the French Navy's submarine * Suffren*.


[[12]](#cite_note-12)On 23 August 2022, she entered the Mediterranean. [13] In December 2022 the ship positioned herself above the 

[TAP pipeline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trans_Adriatic_Pipeline), off the coast of

[Otranto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otranto), Italy. The Italian Navy and other NATO ships immediately moved next to the Academic Pashin ship.


[[14]](#cite_note-14)In June 2024, *Academic Pashin* was part of a flotilla of four Russian vessels that will visit Havana from June 12 to June 17. It sailed close to the East Coast of the United States, while remaining in international waters. [15] As of June 2026, the ship was reported operating with the 

[frigate Admiral Kasatonov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_frigate_Admiral_Kasatonov)in the Mediterranean.
