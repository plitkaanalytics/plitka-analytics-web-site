# No evidence that drone found off Greek coast belongs to Ukrainian operators, Foreign Ministry says

                                        
                            
                        —
                12 May, 20:52

                
                                        Photo: Ekathimerini
                                    

                                                        Heorhii Tykhyi, spokesman for the Ministry of Foreign Affairs of Ukraine, has said there is no evidence that a maritime drone discovered last week off the coast of Greece is linked to Ukrainian operators.

	**Source:** Tykhyi in a comment to journalists; [European Pravda](https://www.eurointegration.com.ua/news/2026/05/12/7237450/)

	**Details:** Tykhyi said Ukraine currently has no information about the uncrewed surface vessel found in Greece.

Advertisement:

	**Quote:** "There is no evidence that it belongs to Ukrainian naval drone operators."

	**More details:** Tykhyi added that Ukraine is open to cooperating with Greece to establish the circumstances of the incident, if the Greek side submits appropriate requests.

	**Background:**

- 
		
			The Guardian [reported](https://www.theguardian.com/world/2026/may/11/greece-explosive-packed-drone-lefkada-western-coast)earlier that Greek authorities have stepped up an investigation into how a drone carrying explosives ended up in waters off the country's west coast.
- Ukrainian defence technology company Uforce clarified in a comment to Ukrainska Pravda that the vessel was not a Magura drone, as some media outlets had previously reported.
- 
		
			Ekathimerini, a Greek media outlet, had [claimed](https://www.ekathimerini.com/politics/foreign-policy/1303182/ukrainian-magura-v5-naval-drone-found-off-lefkada-sparks-investigation/)earlier that a Ukrainian Magura 5 drone had been found in waters south of the Ionian island of Lefkada.
