Russian military-linked roro targeted near Crete
Sam Chambers
September 7, 2026
0
1,491
1 minute read
X
X
Ukraine’s maritime campaign against Russian shipping has pushed deep into the Mediterranean, with reports over the weekend that the sanctioned Russian roro Lady Mariia was struck by drones near Crete.
Drones dropped bombs on the 7,184 dwt vessel on Sunday as it headed east towards Syria. The precise origin of the attack and the extent of any damage remain unclear, while there has been no independent confirmation from either Kyiv or Moscow.
AIS data placed the Russian-flagged ship in the Mediterranean on Saturday after departing Oran, Algeria. Lady Mariia, formerly Stella-Maria, is operated by MG-Flot and has been under US sanctions since 2022. Ukrainian military intelligence lists the ship as being involved in carrying weapons and military equipment between Syria and Russia. Its present cargo has not been independently established.
The weekend also brought a fresh Russian attack on merchant shipping. Moscow’s defence ministry claimed on Sunday that Russian strike drones hit a dry bulk vessel in the Black Sea carrying Western-supplied military equipment for Ukraine. The ship was not identified and Kyiv has yet to confirm the incident.
