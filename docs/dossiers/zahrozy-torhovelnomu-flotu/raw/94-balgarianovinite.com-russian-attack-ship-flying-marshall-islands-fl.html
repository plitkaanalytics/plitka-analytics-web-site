Russian Attack on Ship Flying Marshall Islands Flag in Black Sea Leaves One Dead and Seven Injured 2026

One crew member was killed and seven others were injured aboard a Marshall Islands-flagged vessel following an attack in the Black Sea, Odesa Regional Military Administration Head Oleh Kiper said on his Telegram channel on Tuesday.
“Another Russian attack on civilian vessels in the Black Sea. A Russian drone struck a commercial vessel flying the flag of the Marshall Islands and carrying rapeseed oil. A fire broke out on the upper part of the vessel,” Kiper said.
By deliberately attacking civilian vessels and port infrastructure, Russia is grossly violating international law and posing a threat to global food security, Kiper added.
Ukraine’s latest official census counted more than 150,000 Bulgarians in Odesa Region, making them its third-largest ethnic group. Around 50,000 to 60,000 Bulgarians live in Odesa itself. The largest concentrations of Bulgarians are in the Bolhrad, Izmail and Bilhorod-Dnistrovskyi districts.
