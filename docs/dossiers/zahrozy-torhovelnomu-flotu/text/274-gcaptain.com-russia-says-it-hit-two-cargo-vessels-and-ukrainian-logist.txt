Russia Says It Hit Two Cargo Vessels And Ukrainian Logistics Hubs
Reuters
Total Views: 510
October 25, 2026
MOSCOW, Oct 25 (Reuters) – The Russian Defence Ministry said on Sunday that its drones had
struck a cargo vesse
l bound for the Black Sea port of Chornomorsk in an overnight attack and
another cargo vessel
en route to the port of Odesa during the day.
The ministry said the vessels were being used to supply the Ukrainian army.
Russian forces also hit a logistics center, a distribution center and a logistics terminal in the Ukrainian city of Kharkiv overnight. The ministry said the facilities had been used for storage and distribution of dual-use cargo for the Ukrainian military.
Reuters could not independently verify the Defence Ministry’s information.
