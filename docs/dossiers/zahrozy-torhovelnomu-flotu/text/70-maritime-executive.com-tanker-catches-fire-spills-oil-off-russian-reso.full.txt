Tanker Catches Fire, Spills Oil off Russian Resort Town of Sochi
Spreading, burning pool of oil from a tanker off the coast of Sochi, Oct. 6 (Russian social media)
Published
					Oct 6, 2026 3:10 PM        		by
The Maritime Executive
A large tanker has caught fire off the coast of the resort city of Sochi, Russia, according to local media accounts and satellite sensing data.
The vessel is readily visible from shore, and multiple videos depicting the fire have appeard on Russian social media channels. The bystander-image sequence begins with a substantial fire centered on cargo tanks amidships, followed by obvious signs of burning oil spreading aft down the starboard side. Later videos and photos show a large pool of burning oil on the water aft of the vessel and a towering column of smoke.
Initial reports indicated that the vessel could be the
Aframax Rio,
previously sanctioned by Ukraine for alleged involvement in Russian oil exports. Marine traffic tracking sites last spotted
Aframax Rio
on Monday at a position just off the coast of Sirius, a small coastal town to the southeast of Sochi. NASA FIRMS infrared sensing data shows a large, intense heat signature to the west of Sochi, to the northwest of
Aframax Rio's
last detected position.
Based on satellite imaging, TankerTrackers.com initially identified the vessel as the Russian-flagged tanker
Sphere
(IMO 9323974, ex names
Marble, Creature, Marta 1, LR2 Eternity
), then issued a
correction
and identified the vessel as
Aframax Rio.
