Drones sank two merchant ships and set a third on fire in the Black Sea economic zones of NATO members Romania and Bulgaria on 5 and 6 October, killing at least two seafarers, Romanian and Bulgarian authorities reported. Ukraine blamed Russia for the first attack. Neither Bucharest nor Sofia has said who launched the drones.

Russia has spent months striking ships bound to and from Ukrainian ports while denying responsibility or staying silent. Now merchant vessels are sinking inside NATO members' economic zones without either government naming the attacker, so Moscow pays no political price for hitting allied waters.

### Grain ship sinks off Romania

The Royad Mammadov, a Turkish-owned ship carrying grain from Ukraine's port of Izmail to Italy, sank on 5 October near Romania's Pescăruș offshore platform, 20 nautical miles from the Romanian coast, Digi24 [reported](https://www.digi24.ro/stiri/actualitate/o-nava-ar-fi-fost-lovita-de-o-drona-in-apropierea-neptun-deep-vasul-ar-fi-plecat-din-portul-ismail-3978043). The Romanian Naval Authority described the incident as a fire on board. Romania's Interior Ministry said 11 crew members were rescued and two people died.

Three injured sailors aged 22 to 50 were taken to the Constanța county hospital, conscious and stable, with multiple injuries. Adrian Mihălcioiu, the leader of Romania's Free Seafarers' Union, said the ship was attacked by Russian sea and aerial drones in waters Romanians consider safe.

"

It is a commercial ship, a ship that is not part of the war… people who have nothing to do with the war, it is not their war," he said.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy [blamed](https://t.me/V_Zelenskiy_official/21218) the attack on "two Russian drones" and said the ship was in neutral waters carrying corn. Citizens of Azerbaijan and India were on board, and the captain was killed, he said. Romanian authorities have not attributed the sinking to Russia.

### Two more ships hit in Bulgaria's zone

A day later, drones struck two merchant ships in Bulgaria's exclusive economic zone east of the town of Byala, the first known attack on ships there, Maritime.bg [reported](https://www.maritime.bg/155140/). The Togo-flagged Alfa Watan, sailing from Al-Arish, Egypt, to Sulina, Romania, suffered critical hull damage and sank, Militarnyi [said](https://militarnyi.com/uk/news/bpla-atakuvaly-torgovelni-sudna-bolgariyi/). The Bulgarian ferry Dioscuria found only an overturned lifeboat and life jackets at the scene, and the search for the crew continues, Euronews [reported](https://www.euronews.com/2026/10/06/drones-hit-two-ships-off-coast-of-bulgaria-as-government-calls-emergency-meeting).

The Palau-flagged Able, sailing from Galați, Romania, to Istanbul, caught fire, and the Dioscuria rescued its 18 crew members. Both ships are Turkish-owned, Türkiye Today [reported](https://www.turkiyetoday.com/world/drones-hit-two-turkish-owned-ships-off-bulgaria-one-sinks-crew-missing-3229734). Two sailors with severe injuries were flown to a hospital in Varna, Prime Minister Rumen Radev [said](https://abcnews.com/International/wireStory/bulgarian-pm-drone-attack-2-commercial-vessels-blatant-137025648), according to AP. Sea and aerial drones hit the ships at about 3 a.m., he said, calling the attack "a blatant violation of international law and maritime law."

Radev called an emergency meeting of four ministries, the presidency, and the security services. No one from the Alfa Watan has been found, he [said](https://sofiaglobe.com/2026/10/06/bulgarian-pm-on-drone-attacks-on-ships-in-bulgarias-eez-absolutely-unacceptable/), warning that if the war is not ended in time, it "will continue to expand its spatial scope." The origin of the drones is still being clarified, Maritime.bg said. Several Bulgarian media outlets cited sources as saying the drones were Russian, Sofia Globe [reported](https://sofiaglobe.com/2026/10/06/pm-radev-calls-emergency-meeting-after-drone-attacks-on-two-ships-in-bulgarian-eez-with-one-sunk/).

### Russia's war on Black Sea shipping

Russia had already killed the [captain](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/09/17/russia-kills-captain-of-tanzanian-flagged-ship/) of a Tanzanian-flagged ship in September, and days later it [struck](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/09/25/russia-strikes-second-civilian-ship-in-black-sea-in-six-days-killing-both-captains-to-choke-route-that-carries-90-percent-of-ukraines-farm-exports/) another civilian vessel, killing its captain too. Ukraine says that route carries 90% of its farm exports.

Romania has [destroyed](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/08/20/romania-destroys-a-sea-drone-and-finds-aerial-drone-debris-on-its-soil-amid-russias-attack-on-ukraine/) a sea drone near its Neptun Deep gas project and [found](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/08/12/romania-found-two-russian-drones-bobbing-in-its-sea-and-didnt-wait-to-ask-questions/) Russian drones floating in its economic zone. In September, ISW [linked](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/09/25/isw-connects-drone-crashes-in-moldova-and-romania-a-starlink-fire-in-poland-and-a-berlin-airport-shutdown-into-one-russian-pattern/) drone crashes in Romania and Moldova to a wider Russian pattern of pressure on NATO.

EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas [called](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/09/23/food-must-never-be-used-as-a-weapon-eu-calls-for-black-sea-attack-moratorium/) for a moratorium on Black Sea attacks. Turkish President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan said Ankara was [stepping up](https://www.eurointegration.com.ua/news/2026/09/24/7246227/) diplomacy to restore grain exports. Türkiye and the UN are organizing new Black Sea truce talks between Russia and Ukraine, Bloomberg [reported](https://www.eurointegration.com.ua/news/2026/10/1/7246747/), citing a diplomat. Russia earlier [dismissed](https://euromaidanpress.com/2026/08/14/ukraine-russia-black-sea-truce/) Kyiv's offer of a Black Sea ceasefire.
