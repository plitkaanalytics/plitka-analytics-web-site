A Liberian-flagged oil tanker
caught fire
off Russia’s Black Sea resort city of Sochi on Tuesday evening after what Russian authorities described as an attack by an unmanned vessel, sending flames and thick black smoke over the coastline.
Russia’s Transport Ministry said the Aframax Rio was struck at sea and that crude oil was burning in the Black Sea. All 23 crew members – Indian nationals – were rescued without injuries. Firefighters initially struggled to approach the vessel because of the intensity of the blaze.

Videos and photos circulating on Russian social media showed bright orange flames offshore and a towering column of black smoke visible from Sochi.
The blaze was intense enough to prevent firefighters from immediately tackling it, AFP reported.

Beaches closed as smoke spreads
Authorities restricted access to beaches and sections of the coastline as smoke from the burning tanker spread toward Sochi.
The fire was visible from across the resort city, while emergency services monitored the vessel and surrounding waters.
The incident brought the escalating Black Sea maritime conflict directly to Russia’s southern coast, where shipping and energy infrastructure have increasingly come under threat.
Tanker on Ukraine’s sanctions list
The Aframax Rio has been
identified
by Ukraine as part of Russia’s so-called shadow fleet – a network of tankers used to keep Russian oil exports moving despite Western sanctions, price caps and other restrictions imposed after Moscow’s full-scale invasion.
