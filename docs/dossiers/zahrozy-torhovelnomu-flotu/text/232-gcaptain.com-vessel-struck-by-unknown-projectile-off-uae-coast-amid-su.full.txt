Vessel Struck by Unknown Projectile Off UAE Coast Amid Surge in Attacks, UKMTO Says
Reuters
Total Views: 841
October 9, 2026
LONDON, Oct 9 (Reuters) – A vessel was struck by an unknown projectile 13 nautical miles west of Al Jazeera in the United Arab Emirates on Friday, causing a fire that has since been extinguished, British navy affiliated agency UKMTO said, citing multiple sources.
While there was no claim of responsibility, maritime security sources said on Friday that the strike was outside of the Hormuz area and indicated a widening of risk for ships.
Earlier this week a tanker was struck off the coast of Qatar inside the Gulf.
The status of the crew, extent of damage and environmental impact from the vessel struck on Friday remain unknown, UKMTO said, adding that authorities were investigating the incident.
UKMTO does not typically name vessels, but two shipping and security sources said the vessel struck was the Panama-flagged crude supertanker GEM NO.2.
“GEM NO.2 was struck by an unknown projectile approximately 13nm west of Al Jazeera, UAE, at 1000 (GMT),” British maritime risk management group Vanguard said.
“The vessel reportedly issued a distress alert indicating a fire onboard, which was subsequently extinguished.”
The vessel’s Panama-based operator, listed in shipping databases as Gem No.2 Maritime Corp, could not be reached for comment.
The last position of the tanker, which was fully laden with oil, was at anchor off the UAE coast, LSEG ship tracking data showed on Friday.
