ROYAD MAMMADOV (IMO 9356969, MMSI 341609002) is a General Cargo Ship flagged in Saint Kitts and Nevis. ROYAD MAMMADOV was built in 2006 with a deadweight capacity of 6,933 MT and a length overall of 139 metres. The vessel was last recorded travelling at 7.8 knots, on a heading of 237 degrees, with a next recorded destination of RAVENNA port (ITRAN). Based on MagicPort's screening against 300+ international sanctions watchlists, ROYAD MAMMADOV is currently not sanctioned. MG SHIPPING 5 CO LTD is the registered owner of ROYAD MAMMADOV. SPRING MARINE DENIZCILIK is the commercial manager of ROYAD MAMMADOV. PALMALI GEMICILIK VE ACENTELIK is the ISM manager of ROYAD MAMMADOV.
