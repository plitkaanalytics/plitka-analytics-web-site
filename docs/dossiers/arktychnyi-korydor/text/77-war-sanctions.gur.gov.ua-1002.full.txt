Vessel information
Since May 21, 2025, the EU has imposed sanctions on the vessel for facilitating or supporting actions or policies aimed at the operation, development, or expansion of russia’s energy sector, including energy infrastructure.
Since June 3, 2025, Switzerland has imposed sanctions on the vessel.
On July 20 and August 12, 2025, the EU and Switzerland, respectively, removed the tanker IMO 9958298 from the list of sanctioned vessels.
