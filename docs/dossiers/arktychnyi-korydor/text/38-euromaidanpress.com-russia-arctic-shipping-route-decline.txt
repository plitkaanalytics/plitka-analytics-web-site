Cargo on Russia’s Northern Sea Route fell for the first time since 2022, dropping 2.3% to 37 million tons in 2025, according to an analysis by Russian consultancy Gekon published by ** business daily Kommersant** on 9 February. The analyst behind the study sees no sources of growth for 2026.

**Russia’s northern shipping lane carries the oil and liquefied natural gas that help bankroll Moscow’s war in Ukraine.**

The decline matters beyond the Arctic. Russia’s northern shipping lane carries the oil and liquefied natural gas that help bankroll Moscow’s war in Ukraine—hydrocarbons make up 83% of the route’s cargo. In 2018, ** Putin signed a decree** ordering 80 million metric tons on this route by 2024. Russia delivered less than half, and the number is now going backwards.
