Takeaways
by Bloomberg AI
A sanctioned tanker carrying a gasoline additive from China appears to be heading to Russia along the Northern Sea Route, a rare journey that suggests attacks on its refineries continue to force one of the world’s top fuel exporters to turn to Asia for supplies.
The
Dakar
, a small carrier blacklisted by the European Union earlier this year, is heading west along Russia’s Arctic coast carrying methyl tert-butyl ether, or MTBE, according to Kpler. The chemical, used to improve gasoline quality, was loaded onto another ship — the
Yorik
— in China in early August before being transferred to the Dakar off Yeosu, South Korea, in early September, Kpler data show.
