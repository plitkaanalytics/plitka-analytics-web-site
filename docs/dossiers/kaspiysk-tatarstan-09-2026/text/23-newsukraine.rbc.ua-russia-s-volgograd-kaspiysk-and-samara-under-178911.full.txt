Russia's Volgograd, Kaspiysk and Samara under attack: Details
Fri, September 11, 2026 - 10:05
2 min
Explosions were heard in cities, and fires broke out in some areas
Liliana Oleniak
Photo: An employee of Russia's Ministry of Emergency Situations (Getty Images)
On the night of September 11, several Russian cities, Kaspiysk, Samara, and Volgograd, came under fire; an oil refinery and a port were reportedly attacked, according to OSINT sources.
Volgograd
Volgograd was reportedly hit by a missile attack, as eyewitnesses have also reported. In a video from Volgograd, Russians can be heard swearing as a drone buzzes overhead, followed by the sound of an explosion.
Social media groups, in turn, reported an attack on an oil refinery in Volgograd.
Regional Governor Andrei Bocharov confirmed on the region's Telegram channel that debris had been found on the grounds of an industrial facility in southern Volgograd. Emergency services are on the scene, and cleanup efforts are ongoing.
Kaspiysk
In Kaspiysk, the port was reportedly attacked by a drone strike; public Telegram channels shared several photos of the incident. Early this morning, the city administration posted only advice on how to behave during drone attacks on its Telegram channel; no further information is available at this time.
Samara
Overnight, the city's official Telegram channel warned Samara residents about the drone threat and urged them to be cautious; there have been no other official statements so far.
However, residents reported on public channels that explosions could be heard, and there are reports of air defense systems in operation.
