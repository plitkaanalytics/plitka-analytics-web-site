**Brak Airport** ([IATA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IATA_airport_code): **BCQ**) is an airport serving [Brak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brak,_Libya), the capital of the [Wadi al Shatii District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadi_al_Shatii_District) in [Libya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libya). The airport is in the desert 11 kilometres (6.8 mi) north of the city.

Runway length does not include a 245 metres (804 ft) [displaced threshold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Displaced_threshold) on each end.

The Sebha [VOR-DME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VOR-DME) (Ident: **SEB**) is located 42.7 nautical miles (79.1 km) south-southeast of the airport.[[3]](#cite_note-3)
