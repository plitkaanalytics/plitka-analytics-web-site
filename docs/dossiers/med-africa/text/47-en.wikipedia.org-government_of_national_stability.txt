The **Government of National Stability** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language): حكومة الاستقرار الوطني, [romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanization_of_Arabic): *Ḥukūmat al-istiqrār al-waṭanī*) is a [rival government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rival_government) and [quasi-state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-state) that controls eastern [Libya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libya). [4] It was formed on 3 March 2022, is led by 

[Osama Hammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_Hammad)and supported by the

[House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_(Libya))and the

[Libyan National Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_National_Army). Since its inception, the government has claimed power over Libya in

[competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rival_government)with the Tripoli-based

[Government of National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_National_Unity_(Libya))led by

[Abdul Hamid Dbeibeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Hamid_Dbeibeh), with the

[Libyan Political Dialogue Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_Political_Dialogue_Forum)coordinating the ceasefire agreement.

The Government of National Stability is a nominal civilian government in control of eastern Libya. However, * de facto* control in this area is held by the Libyan National Army and its commander 

[Khalifa Haftar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_Haftar), who controls the government and the region through a

[military dictatorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_dictatorship).


[[5]](#cite_note-Brooke-Holland-7)

[[6]](#cite_note-Oxford-8)As of May 2025 the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations) recognises the [Government of National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_National_Unity_(Libya)) led by [prime minister](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_Minister_of_Libya) [Abdul Hamid Dbeibeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Hamid_Dbeibeh) as the legitimate government of Libya.[[7]](#cite_note-9)

## History

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Government_of_National_Stability&action=edit§ion=1)]

On 21 September 2021, the House of Representatives (HoR), which controls eastern Libya, passed a no-confidence motion against the [Government of National Unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_National_Unity_(Libya)).[[8]](#cite_note-10)

On 10 February 2022, the House of Representatives selected [Fathi Bashagha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fathi_Bashagha) as Prime Minister-designate, after HoR Speaker [Aguila Saleh Issa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aguila_Saleh_Issa) announced the only other candidate, Khalid Al-Baybas, withdrew his candidacy. [9] However, Al-Baybas denied withdrawing from the race.

Prime Minister of the Government of National Unity

[[10]](#cite_note-12)[Abdul Hamid Dbeibeh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdul_Hamid_Dbeibeh)rejected Bashagha's appointment as Prime Minister, stating that he will only hand over power after a national election.


[[9]](#cite_note-:1-11)[LNA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libyan_National_Army)leader

[Khalifa Haftar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khalifa_Haftar)welcomed Bashagha's appointment.


[[11]](#cite_note-13)On 1 March, the House of Representatives voted to give confidence to Bashagha's Government of National Stability.[[12]](#cite_note-:0-14) [13] According to HoR Speaker Saleh, 92 out of 101 attending members voted for the new government.

The

[[12]](#cite_note-:0-14)[High Council of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_Council_of_State_(Libya))rejected "unilateral" steps by the HoR and regards the HoR decision to grant confidence to a new government a violation of the Libyan Political Agreement.

The Government of National Unity refused to transfer powers to the Bashagha government.

[[14]](#cite_note-16)The

[[15]](#cite_note-17)[United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations)has voiced concerns over the vote due to reports on lack of transparency and procedure, and acts of intimidation prior to the HoR session.


[[16]](#cite_note-18)Bashagha and his cabinet were sworn in at the House of Representatives headquarters in [Tobruk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tobruk) on 3 March.[[17]](#cite_note-:22-19)[[18]](#cite_note-20)

In mid-2022, the [two governments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rival_government) functioned in parallel, holding [dual power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_power).[[19]](#cite_note-21)

There were [clashes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Tripoli_clashes) between supporters of the two governments starting in May 2022, [20] which escalated on 27 August 2022.


[[21]](#cite_note-23)In May 2023 Bashagha was replaced by Finance Minister [Osama Hamada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osama_Hamada), citing poor performance.[[22]](#cite_note-24)

A coalition of lawmakers is putting pressure on the House of Representatives in November 2025 to remove President Aguila Saleh from his position as president.[[23]](#cite_note-25)
