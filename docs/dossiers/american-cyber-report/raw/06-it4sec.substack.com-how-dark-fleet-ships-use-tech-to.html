How “dark fleet” ships use tech to bypass monitoring, law enforcement takeovers - and even self-destruct 🚢🛢️🥷🏻၊၊||၊💥

Denis Laskov
Sep 23, 2026

Security researchers Kenny Miltenberger and Shane Cancilla share some very interesting details on how ships in the “dark fleet” that are used to bypass sanctions use technology to stay hidden, protected from tracking, and ready for takeover.

The research was done by U.S. Coast Guard personnel, so this is a rare opportunity to learn the tricks used by shady ship owners. Some are obvious (like using two AIS transponders), some are very technical (like patching firmware), and some are quite creative (like covering an antenna to prevent transmission).

And of course, remote “drop the cargo” or “remote explosion” features for oil tankers with people on them and nature around are state-of-the-art evil tech...
