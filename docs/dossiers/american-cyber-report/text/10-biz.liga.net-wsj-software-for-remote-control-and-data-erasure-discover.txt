# WSJ: Software for remote control and data erasure discovered on shadow fleet vessels

[Vira Kasiyan](https://www.liga.net/en/author/vera-kasiyan)

Programs for remote access to ship systems and even attempts to remotely delete data were discovered on tankers of the so-called shadow fleet, which transport sanctioned oil from Russia and Iran, after the ships were detained by American security forces. About this [writes](https://www.wsj.com/articles/the-dangerous-tech-found-aboard-dark-fleet-tankers-captured-by-the-u-s-34762a3a) The Wall Street Journal.

This is an investigation based on materials from the US Coast Guard's cyber units, which investigated [captivated](https://news.liga.net/en/politics/news/the-united-states-intercepts-a-tanker-of-irans-shadow-fleet-the-37th-of-those-under-us-sanctions) or detained tankers.

The vast majority of the world's shadow fleet tankers belong to Russia. The aggressor country uses this network to circumvent Western sanctions and export oil. Attacks on such vessels could increase pressure on one of the key sectors that finances the war against Ukraine. That, [how exactly does the West plan to strike at the Russian "shadow fleet"?](https://www.liga.net/en/politics/articles/britain-plans-to-send-russian-money-from-the-shadow-fleet-to-ukraine-how-it-will-work) and turn Russian oil money into aid to Ukraine, – read the analysis on [ LIGA.net](https://www.liga.net/ua).

"We have long known that the Shadow Fleet is a significant physical threat because they [operate old ships](https://news.liga.net/en/world/news/ft-more-than-half-of-shadow-fleet-tankers-could-cause-environmental-disaster)" by not maintaining them properly. But until these inspections, we didn't know exactly what cyber threats were hidden aboard these vessels," said Rear Admiral Jason Tama, head of the US Coast Guard Cyber Command.

It turns out that ships are often equipped with communication systems that provide a constant connection to the Internet. Remote desktop access programs, such as AnyDesk and TeamViewer, are also used. This allows tanker owners and operators to remotely intervene in the operation of ship systems.

In addition, instructions for falsifying GPS data, modified network cables and equipment for transmitting false coordinates, and several AIS (Automatic Identification System) systems that allowed ships to broadcast various identification data were discovered.

The most disturbing episode, according to the WSJ, was an attempt by administrators of one of the ships to remotely delete data after US representatives boarded it.

Pirated software infected with malware has been found on some tankers. The infected computers are connected to the ship's critical operating and navigation systems.

"For a ship carrying tens of millions of gallons of crude oil, which is very volatile, there is always a risk of fire and explosion. The atmosphere in the tanks needs to be very carefully controlled to prevent fire and explosion. And there is always a risk of oil spills," Tama explained.

The WSJ report suggests that the shadow fleet is not just a collection of old tankers that help circumvent sanctions. According to US intelligence, it is a well-organized system with elements of cyber manipulation, remote control, and evidence concealment.

The publication of this data, the publication notes, should encourage US allies to tighten control over such vessels. Some countries, in particular France, [Great Britain](https://biz.liga.net/en/transport/news/uk-detention-of-russian-shadow-fleet-tanker-could-mark-sanctions-turning-point) and Germany have already begun to more actively inspect tankers involved in the transportation of sanctioned oil.

Russians use shadow fleet to circumvent sanctions[sell resources](https://biz.liga.net/en/energy/news/reuters-russia-plans-to-cut-oil-exports-in-june) and use the funds received for the war against Ukraine. At the same time, against the backdrop of the conflict in the Middle East, Moscow got a chance to make money on oil due to rising prices. In return, Ukrainian defenders [successfully beat](https://www.liga.net/en/politics/articles/ukraine-has-gained-a-strategic-advantage-in-the-war-die-welt) on the economic and military infrastructure in the aggressor country.
